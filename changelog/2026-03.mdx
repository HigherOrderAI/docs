March was the heaviest evidence month. SOC 2, ISO 27001, and VAPT analysis all landed, and an Overall Risk Analysis route now combines them into a single grade per vendor. The Vendor Intake wizard with live tier-based risk scoring shipped, Slack became a first-class surface, and Risk Reports gained DOCX and PDF export.
