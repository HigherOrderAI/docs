January was a foundational month — the assessment bot that joins Zoom calls, voice transcription during risk-assessment conversations, and the first version of AI threat modeling against vendors. Most of the platform surfaces visible today were still being scaffolded. The user-facing capabilities arrive in February.

### New
- AI Threat Modeling capability (STRIDE-based)
- Multi-phase question support in assessment bot (Q1/Q2/Phase-B sequencing)
- Manual voice controls from the browser during bot calls
- Audio/video publish to Zoom from the assessment bot
- Threat models now viewable on each vendor

### Improved
- Pre-defined voice messages and STRIDE analysis output
