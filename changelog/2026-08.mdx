August centers on the vendor-facing experience and on connecting HigherOrder to the systems your teams already use. The Vendor Portal is rebuilt on a lighter, clearer design, and SharePoint Online joins the integrations for document ingestion. Risk Scoring reaches V0.5 with per-option scores and a normalized 0–100 rating, admins get user-level role management and email branding, and the platform can now ingest third-party risk reports such as Rubix. Mid-month, questionnaire import closes the loop for vendors who answer by email: an uploaded workbook becomes a standard assessment rather than a read-only analysis.

## Week of Aug 10

### New
- Questionnaire Import V1.0 — upload a vendor's completed Excel questionnaire and it becomes a standard assessment: imported answers run through the same evaluation, review, findings, and evidence pipeline as answers submitted through the Vendor Portal. The import matches the workbook against your existing questionnaire templates — every matching template is shown, and a match is reused rather than duplicated — with ambiguous question matches adjudicated by the model, and reviewer comments embedded in the workbook carried in as answer context
- Adjust assessment scope after launch — add questionnaires or domains to an in-flight assessment, or withdraw domains and sub-domains, with a mandatory justification the vendor sees; withdrawn answers are kept but stop counting toward progress and the risk score
- Observation grouping — group related observations within a TPRM report

### Improved
- Vendor Portal bulk submission — answer and submit groups of questions at once
- Review workbench navigation — the domain panel shows vendor-answered counts per domain, selection works at the sub-domain level, and contradictions are listed alongside gaps
- Review workbench rows and the question detail pane show additional answer context
- Questionnaire domains listed in a left-hand panel for quicker navigation across questionnaires
- Third-party risk report analysis adds an outsourcing compliance rating, with statutory and screening checks
- The assessment risk score is now also computed when the vendor submits, not only at completion
- Vendor inventory keeps the selected tab when you navigate back, and assessment and questionnaire tabs share a unified status
- The engagement header now shows the Material chip alongside Category and Outsourcing

### Fixed
- Evidence evaluation guardrails — oversized inputs are no longer sent to the model

## Week of Aug 3

### New
- Vendor Portal redesign — the vendor-facing surface rebuilt on a lighter theme, with a redesigned customer review page, questions grouped hierarchically by domain, each finding's latest status shown inline, and a clearer progress bar; "AI Review" is now "AI Analysis"
- SharePoint Online integration — connect a SharePoint Online site or folder via app-only credentials (no OAuth redirect) and sync documents into HigherOrder, including `.xlsx` and `.csv`; folder-scoped sync with a folder picker. See [Integrations](/administration/integrations)
- Risk Scoring V0.5 — assign a numeric score to each answer option; on assessment completion the platform computes a normalized 0–100 risk score and a banded rating. See [Risk Scoring Methodology](/concepts/risk-scoring)
- Third-party risk report ingestion — ingest an outsourcing / vendor risk report (such as Rubix) and surface it on a dedicated Outsourcing tab, enabled by tenant config, with standardized display names
- User Role Management — assign and remove roles on users directly from the admin user table; a "View all engagements" permission for business users
- AI-suggested tab (Vendor Portal) — a lens showing every question for which document analysis has proposed an answer from uploaded evidence
- Email branding — brand-color settings in Tenant Settings for outbound email
- Doc links V1.0 — in-app links to methodology documentation (e.g. the outsourcing methodology)

### Improved
- Customer review controls — customers see only submitted or N/A answers (a vendor can override an N/A with a manual input); customer queries are surfaced prominently; select options lay out vertically; partial matches show an excerpt, and an answer that scores above 90 without a defined or met expectation is marked **partial** and held out of bulk auto-approval
- Vendor Portal shows document upload and analysis progress
- Assessment emails to the business contact work with or without a vendor contact on file
- Question numbering in questionnaire templates; keyboard navigation (←/→) through questions on the portal
- Classification gains Privacy and AppSec applicable flags

### Fixed
- Wrong banner shown after closing a Risk Assessment
- Next action shown incorrectly when a Risk Assessment is in progress on a parallel engagement
- Question ID shown twice per row; sidebar overlaying popups
