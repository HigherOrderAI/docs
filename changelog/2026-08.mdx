August centers on the vendor-facing experience and on connecting HigherOrder to the systems your teams already use. The Vendor Portal is rebuilt on a lighter, clearer design, and SharePoint Online — followed by OneDrive for Business and Google Drive — joins the integrations for document ingestion. Risk Scoring reaches V0.5 with per-option scores and a normalized 0–100 rating, and questionnaire import closes the loop for vendors who answer by email. In the back half of the month, the entity-level threat assessment becomes the default for every vendor, the Service Risk Profile gains a pre-submit consistency check, and a completed Risk Assessment exports as a controls-format report.

## Week of Aug 24

### New
- Google Drive integration — sync vendor documents from selected Drive folders into the standard document pipeline. Connect with your own Google OAuth client (read-only scope), pick folders in a lazy tree spanning My Drive, shared folders, and shared drives — or paste a folder link — and fetch on demand. Google-native files convert on the way in (Docs → Word, Sheets → Excel, Slides → PDF), and each run produces a per-folder sync report. Disconnecting revokes access at Google and keeps ingested documents. See [Integrations](/administration/integrations)
- OneDrive for Business integration — sync vendor documents from specific users' OneDrive accounts into the standard document pipeline. OneDrive shares the Microsoft 365 connection with SharePoint (no new app registration or admin consent); access is granted per account with two ready-to-run Graph Explorer steps, scope is per account (whole drive or selected folders), and each fetch produces a per-account sync report. See [Integrations](/administration/integrations)
- Risk Assessment report V0.1 — download a completed Risk Assessment as a controls-format PDF from the **Report** button on the engagement header: one row per scored question with its control objective, domain, and Compliant / Non-Compliant / Not Applicable status. A dropdown on the button lists every completed RA cycle, newest first
- Engagement intake editing — the intake-captured fields (name, product/service, use case, consuming entities, notes) are editable from the engagement's Overview tab, with every change in the audit trail. The vendor record can be renamed — the dialog states how many engagements and assessments the rename touches — or the engagement repointed to a different vendor record while no assessment or SRP submission exists yet
- Post-onboarding tracker — the engagement's progress strip now continues past onboarding, with one node per periodic assessment cycle (Pending / Triggered / Ongoing / Completed, year-qualified once there are several) and hover detail showing the questionnaire, dates, and risk rating

### Improved
- Closed-form answers (yes/no, single- and multi-select) with a defined expected answer are now judged deterministically in audited-reports-sufficient mode — a matching selection auto-verifies with no model call; a shortfall is held for a reviewer with the unticked options listed as gaps
- Domain scores appear in the review workbench rail from the vendor's first scored answer — per-domain chips no longer wait for the overall score's coverage threshold
- TPRM observation contradictions now cite every question involved — number, section, full untruncated text, and the vendor's answer — with a deep link into the review workbench
- Question numbers in questionnaire templates are stable: reordering, re-categorizing, nesting, or deleting questions no longer renumbers the rest, so numbers quoted to vendors or matched to an incumbent system stay put
- SRP consistency findings show each cited question's full text and answer, not just its code
- Choice questions can no longer be saved with an expected answer that matches none of their options

### Fixed
- A closed-form answer with no configured expected answer no longer self-approves into the bulk-approval bucket — it is held for a human decision
- Returning from an engagement detail page restores the correct list tab

## Week of Aug 17

### New
- Threat assessment v2 is now the default for every vendor — a single entity-level assessment built from all of a vendor's documents, run automatically on vendor creation and document ingestion (no architecture diagram required). The vendor profile shows threats ranked by risk band with expandable DREAD detail, a Controls tab split into buyer, vendor, and SOC 2 complementary user-entity controls, a Vendor Questions tab tied to the scores that depend on each answer, and a new **Evidence** tab — the security fact sheet, every fact graded and linked to its source document
- SRP consistency check V1 — submitting the Service Risk Profile now runs a cross-answer contradiction check (deterministic rules plus a model fallback). Each conflict is resolved by revisiting the answers or keeping them with a note that is stored with the submission; conflicts acknowledged on an earlier submit stay suppressed while the cited answers are unchanged. The check advises — it never blocks a submit
- Scoped assessment launches — an engagement plan can launch selected domains or sub-domains of a standard questionnaire instead of a separately maintained mini-template; narrow scopes (e.g. staffing-only or privacy-only reviews) now send a familiar questionnaire showing just the relevant domains

### Improved
- Unsuitable VAPT uploads — a program summary, policy, or attestation letter uploaded as a pen-test report now gets an explanatory verdict card: what the document actually is, which signals of a real test report are missing, and upload/regenerate actions — instead of an empty analysis
- Engagement Findings tab — a status triage bar (In Remediation / Open / Closed), closing findings in place with an AI-drafted resolution note composed from the vendor's own updates, and downloadable attachments on vendor updates
- Vendor Portal findings — submitting an update confirms the status change and offers a jump to the next open finding; due-date chips turn amber 14 days out and red only when overdue
- SharePoint setup — the folder picker echoes the resolved site ("Connected to …") and warns when the URL matched only the tenant root; the grant panel shows two ready-to-paste Graph Explorer steps, derived server-side from the site-collection root, and appears only once a site URL is entered
- Criticality and per-question weights are gone from questionnaire authoring — the template's Scoring page is the single owner of the weights risk scoring uses
- A PDF that wraps embedded attachments (a cover letter carrying a trust package) now ingests as N+1 documents — each embedded file is extracted, classified, and analyzed in its own right, on every ingestion path
- Long audited reports are evaluated in full — the per-document text budget for evidence evaluation rose from 250K to 400K characters, covering full-length SOC 2 Type 2 reports including the test-results matrix
- Evidence quoted from several documents keeps its verified snippets even when one quote fails verification, instead of the whole answer collapsing to "no evidence found"
- Vendor document uploads (Add Vendor dialog and the Documents tab) share a consistent 10 MB per-file cap with matching messages
- Data Localization, Outsourcing, and DPSC ride-along questionnaires launch only on Category 1 risk assessments — a Category 2 engagement receives exactly its configured scope

### Fixed
- SharePoint site resolution honors the full pasted URL — a subsite under the root site collection no longer falls back silently to the tenant root, and an unresolvable URL fails visibly at Test connection instead of syncing the whole site
- Sub-question answers are evaluated with their parent's answer as context
- Evidence text containing special characters is no longer skipped during evaluation
- OCR text extraction fixes for scanned documents
- Stale questionnaire status badges on assessment lists

## Week of Aug 10

### New
- Questionnaire Import V1.0 — upload a vendor's completed Excel questionnaire and it becomes a standard assessment: imported answers run through the same evaluation, review, findings, and evidence pipeline as answers submitted through the Vendor Portal. The import matches the workbook against your existing questionnaire templates — every matching template is shown, and a match is reused rather than duplicated — with ambiguous question matches adjudicated by the model, and reviewer comments embedded in the workbook carried in as answer context
- Adjust assessment scope after launch — add questionnaires or domains to an in-flight assessment, or withdraw domains and sub-domains, with a mandatory justification the vendor sees; withdrawn answers are kept but stop counting toward progress and the risk score
- Observation grouping — group related observations within a TPRM report
- Questionnaire assignments — on an assessment with two or more questionnaires, assign each questionnaire to a team member from its chip in the header strip; the assignee receives an email deep-linked to the review workbench pre-filtered to their questionnaire. Assignment routes the work without locking anyone out

### Improved
- Vendor Portal bulk submission — answer and submit groups of questions at once
- Review workbench navigation — the domain panel shows vendor-answered counts per domain, selection works at the sub-domain level, and contradictions are listed alongside gaps
- Review workbench rows and the question detail pane show additional answer context
- Questionnaire domains listed in a left-hand panel for quicker navigation across questionnaires
- Third-party risk report analysis adds an outsourcing compliance rating, with statutory and screening checks
- The assessment risk score is now also computed when the vendor submits, not only at completion
- Vendor inventory keeps the selected tab when you navigate back, and assessment and questionnaire tabs share a unified status
- The engagement header now shows the Material chip alongside Category and Outsourcing
- Vendor Portal and review workbench performance — faster list views and a workbench detail page that loads only what it needs

### Fixed
- Evidence evaluation guardrails — oversized inputs are no longer sent to the model

## Week of Aug 3

### New
- Vendor Portal redesign — the vendor-facing surface rebuilt on a lighter theme, with a redesigned customer review page, questions grouped hierarchically by domain, each finding's latest status shown inline, and a clearer progress bar; "AI Review" is now "AI Analysis"
- SharePoint Online integration — connect a SharePoint Online site or folder via app-only credentials (no OAuth redirect) and sync documents into HigherOrder, including `.xlsx` and `.csv`; folder-scoped sync with a folder picker. See [Integrations](/administration/integrations)
- Risk Scoring V0.5 — assign a numeric score to each answer option; on assessment completion the platform computes a normalized 0–100 risk score and a banded rating. See [Risk Scoring Methodology](/concepts/risk-scoring)
- Third-party risk report ingestion — ingest an outsourcing / vendor risk report (such as Rubix) and surface it on a dedicated Outsourcing tab, enabled by tenant config, with standardized display names
- User Role Management — assign and remove roles on users directly from the admin user table; a "View all engagements" permission for business users
- AI-suggested tab (Vendor Portal) — a lens showing every question for which document analysis has proposed an answer from uploaded evidence
- Email branding — brand-color settings in Tenant Settings for outbound email
- Doc links V1.0 — in-app links to methodology documentation (e.g. the outsourcing methodology)

### Improved
- Customer review controls — customers see only submitted or N/A answers (a vendor can override an N/A with a manual input); customer queries are surfaced prominently; select options lay out vertically; partial matches show an excerpt, and an answer that scores above 90 without a defined or met expectation is marked **partial** and held out of bulk auto-approval
- Vendor Portal shows document upload and analysis progress
- Assessment emails to the business contact work with or without a vendor contact on file
- Question numbering in questionnaire templates; keyboard navigation (←/→) through questions on the portal
- Classification gains Privacy and AppSec applicable flags

### Fixed
- Wrong banner shown after closing a Risk Assessment
- Next action shown incorrectly when a Risk Assessment is in progress on a parallel engagement
- Question ID shown twice per row; sidebar overlaying popups
