title: "November 2026"
description: "Workflow designer, SLA & Performance tracking, Jira ticket comments, vendor page regroup, .xlsm evidence, activity report PDF"
---
November so far makes the engagement workflow itself configurable. Administrators get a **workflow designer** and an **attribute registry** under Administration, so the steps an engagement moves through, the attributes it is classified on, and the labels those attributes carry on screen are tenant data rather than fixed behaviour. Engagements gain an **SLA & Performance** tab that tracks availability and performance readings month by month — typed in, read from an uploaded report, or submitted by the vendor through a signed link — and a reviewer can raise a finding from any reading. Engagements that began as a Jira ticket now report their milestones back to that ticket as comments. The vendor side of the product is simplified: the vendor list is cut to six columns built on engagement-based criticality, the vendor tier is removed, and the vendor page is regrouped from twelve tabs to seven around an Overview that lists the vendor's engagements and assessments. Macro-enabled Excel files are accepted wherever documents are uploaded, and an engagement's activity trail downloads as a PDF.

## Week of Nov 16

### New
- **Vendor page regroup** — the vendor page has seven tabs instead of twelve: Overview, Documents, Document Analysis, Threat Model, Continuous Monitoring, OSS Threats, and 4th Parties. Controls, Vendor Questions, and the fact sheet are sub-tabs of Threat Model; VAPT is a sub-tab of Document Analysis; Policy Watch is a sub-tab of Continuous Monitoring. **Overview** carries the vendor note, the vendor's **Engagements** (the same table as the engagement list, scoped to this vendor), and its **Assessments** — the former Questionnaires tab with its upload action, now including engagement assessments and an Owner column, showing the latest five with *View all*. Old links to a moved tab open its new place. See [Vendor Inventory](/vendor-risk/inventory)
- **Vendor rating from engagements** — the vendor page header shows the worst risk tier across the vendor's counted engagements, named and coloured as on the engagement list. It replaces the document-analysis band in the header; that band's facts now sit on the Overall Risk grade card under Document Analysis
- **Engagement activity report** — a PDF of an engagement's Activity tab: a summary block and count tiles, the timeline oldest first (when, action, by whom, assessment, notes and documents), and the emails sent (when, which mail, read by, recipients, delivery — no email bodies). Download it from the **Report** menu on the engagement header or the **Download** button on the Activity tab; either gives the whole report whatever filter is on screen. Times are printed in your browser's time zone, which the PDF names. The report carries the most recent 1,000 timeline entries and 1,000 emails and says so when there are more; text outside the Latin alphabet prints as "?"
- **Vendor lookup from the vendor page** — **New Engagement** on a vendor page opens the wizard with the vendor chosen, domain, CISO and address filled from the vendor record, and the same vendor lookup running that picking the vendor from the list runs

### Improved
- **Vendor list** — six columns: Vendor name, Status, Criticality, Engagements, Last assessed, and Actions. **Criticality** is a Critical / Non-Critical chip taken from the vendor's engagements — Critical when any counted engagement is classified critical, a dash when none is classified — and is sortable and filterable (Critical, Non-Critical, Not classified). **Engagements** counts those in progress, onboarded, on hold, or offboarding; **Last assessed** is the latest completed assessment on any of the vendor's engagements. The cards are Total, Active, and Critical. Tier, Assessment Risk, Annual Value, Renewal Date, and Category columns, the Tier and Assessment Risk filters, and the Awaiting Vendor card are gone. The Vendors dashboard's **Critical vendors** tile uses the same rule and drills into the list filtered to match
- **Vendor tier removed** — nothing derived the tier, and criticality now comes from engagements. The tier badge leaves the vendor page (the criticality chip takes its place), the **Vendor Tier** field leaves the questionnaire template form and cards, and the New Vendor Assessment dialog offers every questionnaire once a vendor is picked instead of matching template tier to vendor tier. A saved custom dashboard that charts vendors by tier shows its error banner until that chart is changed. The engagement's SRP risk tier is a separate value and is unchanged
- **Regulatory Compliance dashboard retired** — its posture and control score were a lookup on vendor tier. The tab is removed from [Dashboards](/dashboards) and from the dashboard export; an old link to it opens the first dashboard
- **Policy Watch tab shown by default** — the vendor page's Continuous Monitoring → Policy Watch tab no longer waits on a feature flag. Monitoring itself is still switched on per tenant under Administration → Tenant Settings → **Policy Watch**, off by default
- The Threat Model and Continuous Monitoring tabs on the vendor page can be hidden per tenant. HigherOrder sets this for a tenant on request; there is no switch in Administration. Hiding a tab does not stop the analysis behind it

### Fixed
- The vendor page's tab strip no longer snaps back to the active tab while its scrollbar is being dragged

## Week of Nov 9

### New
- **Macro-enabled Excel (.xlsm) evidence** — `.xlsm` files, the format HECVAT and SIG questionnaires usually ship in, are accepted and read like `.xlsx`: cell values only, macros are never run. This covers Add Evidence and vendor onboarding, the vendor portal, the review workbench, questionnaire import, questionnaire template upload, files fetched from a vendor's trust center, and SharePoint and OneDrive sync. A file renamed to `.xlsm` that is not a real workbook is refused

### Improved
- **One file-type list for every document picker** — every picker and every upload route now reads the same list, so a file a picker accepts is one the platform can read. Vendor document upload gains CSV, Markdown, JSON, and XML; the vendor portal gains TXT, CSV, Markdown, JSON, and XML; questionnaire import and template upload take `.xlsx`, `.xlsm`, `.xls`, and `.csv`. Typed report slots (VAPT, contract, architecture, SBOM) take documents only — PDF, DOCX, DOC, TXT, MD
- SharePoint and OneDrive connections sync PDF, Word, plain text, Markdown, Excel (including `.xlsm`), and CSV by default; JSON and XML can be ticked on the connection form, and images are never synced. A connection left on the default follows it as the default changes. See [Integrations](/administration/integrations)
- Paste-a-URL document fetch now skips files the platform cannot read (presentations, archives) instead of creating failed documents, and trust-center fetch picks up CSV, TXT, and Markdown files and no longer fetches presentations

### Fixed
- An intake started from chat saved its AI-usage answer incorrectly, so the request lost its AI-usage risk points
- In the new-request wizard, **Submit anyway** stays visible after *Apply suggestion*, and Submit no longer runs the validation a second time

## Week of Nov 2

### New
- **Workflow designer** — Administration → **Workflows** lists the tenant's engagement workflows (onboarding, offboarding) with their revision. A workflow opens read-only until an administrator edits it; edits are held in a draft, checked with **Validate**, and go live on **Publish**, which raises the revision. Each step is configured in place — including the Classification step's rules that derive an engagement's attributes from its risk profile answers, and recurrence rules that set the reassessment interval from an attribute's value. The risk profile form editor now lives inside the designer; the old form-editor link redirects there
- **Attribute registry** — Administration → **Attributes** lists every attribute the tenant classifies engagements on, each with its label, options, role, and display settings: position and tone among the engagement header chips, and whether it can be filtered on, exported, and shown in emails. The engagement header chips, the filter sheet and its chips, the export column picker, the SRP risk card, the pre-onboarding coverage dialog, the Engagements dashboard, and the classification email take their labels from the registry, so renaming an attribute there renames it everywhere. Some filter and export labels changed to their registry wording as a result (the export header "SRP tier" reads "SRP Risk Tier")
- **SLA & Performance tracking** — a new **SLA & Performance** tab on the engagement shows monthly and quarterly grids of readings for each tracked metric, against its target. Two metric types ship: **availability** (unplanned and planned downtime minutes computed to an uptime percentage, optionally within a service window) and **performance** (a statistic and its value). A reading can be typed in, or read from a dropped screenshot, PDF, or sheet — the file is read for that metric and period and kept as the reading's source; several files for one period go through a review step (matched, missing, unmatched) before anything is saved. **Raise finding** turns a reading into an *SLA breach* finding that carries the metric, period, value, and target. See [SLA & Performance](/sla-performance)
- **Request SLA report** — from the SLA & Performance tab, mail a vendor contact a signed link (valid 30 days) to a page where they upload the period's report, review what was read, and submit — no portal sign-in needed. Vendor-reported values are tinted in the grid. Two new rules, for the request and the submission, appear under Administration → [Notifications](/administration/notifications)
- **Metrics catalog** — Administration → **Metrics** holds the tenant's metric definitions: which engagements each applies to, its target, its service window, and retire / reactivate. An engagement can also carry metrics of its own, added from its tab. Administrators can load readings for many engagements at once with **Upload SLA readings** beside Export on the engagement list — download the template, upload the sheet, inspect what it will do, then save
- **Jira ticket comments** — an engagement created from an uploaded Jira ticket posts a short comment on that ticket at each milestone: intake submitted, classified, assessment sent, assessment submitted, review complete (with or without findings), extension requested and decided, and completed. HigherOrder only comments — it never creates a ticket. Comments post for a tenant with Jira connected under [Integrations](/administration/integrations); a connection made before this release has to be disconnected and reconnected once to grant comment permission
- **Jira switch in Notifications** — in Administration → [Notifications](/administration/notifications), each rule that posts a Jira comment has its own **Jira** switch beside Email, on the row and in the edit panel, which also shows where the comment goes, when one was last posted, and the comment with sample values. A Jira switch-off is marked as a change and is covered by Revert. The Activity trail gains a Jira filter listing each comment with its ticket key and, for a refused comment, the reason. A banner says when Jira is not connected

### Improved
- **Merged launch mode by default** — a tenant that has never chosen an assessment launch mode now launches merged: every questionnaire the engagement's rules match goes to the vendor as one assessment and one invite. Tenants that set a mode keep it. Change it under Administration → Tenant Settings → **Assessment launch mode**. See [Vendor Assessments](/vendor-risk/assessments)
- **US baseline content, version 2** — a newly provisioned US tenant starts with a 23-question risk profile form, 20 engagement attributes derived from its answers, onboarding and offboarding workflows, and four questionnaires: security (79 questions in 17 categories), privacy (21 in 9), and vendor and internal offboarding checklists. Reassessment falls every 12 months for Critical and High engagements and every 24 otherwise. Tenants already provisioned are not changed
- **Default risk scoring for the US questionnaires** — the security and privacy questionnaires in the US baseline now arrive scored (55 of 79 and 16 of 21 questions) with Low 0–10 / Medium 11–15 / High 16–100 bands on every top-level category, so assessments on a newly provisioned tenant end with a rating instead of unrated. Scores, weights, and bands stay editable on the template's Scoring page. See [Risk Scoring](/concepts/risk-scoring)
- On a newly provisioned US tenant, post-onboarding reassessments use their own Risk Assessment templates, so they count as RAs — not PQs — in the engagement list columns, the dashboards, and assignment routing
- The engagement header shows a Jira ticket key as one chip instead of two

### Fixed
- **SRP stays on the form version it was first saved on** — after a tenant edited its risk profile form, a submitted SRP was shown against the newest version, so questions and answers no longer lined up ("15 of 23 answered", or no questions at all). An engagement's SRP now keeps the form version that was current at its first draft or submit, for viewing, editing, resubmitting, and AI suggestions. Later form changes apply to engagements that have not yet saved an SRP
- **One assessment per questionnaire** — when an engagement's plan routed one questionnaire through several rules, parallel launch mode sent the vendor a separate assessment per rule; it now sends one per questionnaire with the scopes combined. In both modes, the launch, post-onboarding, and coverage dialogs kept only the last rule's scope for such a questionnaire, so the vendor could be sent a fraction of the planned questions; the dialogs now keep every part
- A questionnaire template used only by an offboarding workflow could be archived or have its slug changed, after which the next offboarding launch failed. The change is now refused, naming the workflow and step that use the template
