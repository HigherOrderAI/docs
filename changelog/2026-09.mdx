September is about running the programme, not just the assessment. **My Work** becomes the landing page — every analyst's open items in one list, with AI-drafted follow-ups sent from the platform — and engagement requests and questionnaires are assigned automatically by kind and by load. **TPRM Dashboards** reach V1.1, with every number drilling into the list that produced it, and the engagement list is redesigned around what the dashboards report. Administrators get a **Notifications** tab that governs every mail the platform sends, and an **Evidence policy** tab that decides which documents count as attested evidence. On the vendor side, a pre-submit **Final check** catches contradictions before the risk team ever sees them, and a weekday email lists the questions the risk team is still waiting on. Underneath: the DPSC control crosswalk judges control-mapped questions from the audit report's tested controls, the outsourcing report is realigned to the RBI risk definitions, and the pre-onboarding questionnaire is asked once per vendor.

## Week of Sep 14

### New
- **My Work** — the new landing page. Every user sees their own list — *Waiting on me*, *Waiting on others*, *Coming up* — built from sixteen kinds of work (a questionnaire submitted or gone quiet, an observation report awaiting confirmation, a finding overdue, a business review pending, a due-diligence report missing or expired, a request waiting on triage, a reassessment coming up). Admins and managers land on **Team Work**: summary tiles, who holds what, items nobody owns, and items held past the team's threshold. Every row has a **Draft follow-up** that writes the vendor chase from the engagement's own facts; send it from HigherOrder or open it in Gmail. A Monday and Thursday morning digest with the same figures goes to TPRM managers. See [My Work](/my-work)
- **Automatic assignment** — every new engagement request gets a TPRM owner on arrival: the active analyst with the least open work. Managers reassign from the triage drawer, and the requests list gains a TPRM Owner column and filter (Mine / Unassigned / a person). Questionnaires are routed by kind on every launch path via a new **Assignment** tenant setting — per kind, the engagement owner (default), one named person, or a pool balanced by least open work. Routing never blocks a launch, and automatic assignments send no email
- **Email vendor** from the engagement page's actions menu — opens the same drafting panel as a My Work row, pre-filled with the engagement's questionnaire facts and recent thread; sends under the tenant's identity and files in **Activity → Email**. When nothing is waiting on the vendor, the panel opens with the facts and an empty message and no model call is made
- **Weekday questions email to the vendor** — Monday to Friday at 17:00 tenant time, one mail per engagement listing the questions the risk team has asked in the portal that the vendor has not yet answered (oldest first, up to ten, the rest counted). The button opens the portal on the **Customer asked** tab. Whoever asked is on CC. On by default; switch off or change the time under Administration → Notifications
- **Awaiting vendor reply** — the review page marks each question the risk team asked and the vendor has not answered with a teal chip ("asked 3 days ago") and a new **Awaiting reply** filter pill; My Work lists the same questions per questionnaire under *Waiting on others* with its own team threshold ("Vendor question unanswered", default three days). The **ETA Extensions** pill and card are retired
- **Evidence policy** — Administration → Tenant Settings → **Evidence policy**: a grid of document categories against five rules (validity months, grace days, what happens when expired, when undated, and whether a mention in the document is sufficient), with per-document-type exceptions and an "effective policy for" preview. In audited-reports-sufficient mode, the document types that count as attested evidence now come from this policy instead of a fixed SOC 2 / VAPT list — policies count by default, certificates and attestation letters do not. See [Administration](/administration#evidence-policy)
- **SecurityPal trust centers** — supported natively alongside SafeBase: public documents are pulled on discovery, and a data-room approval email pasted on the vendor's Documents tab (or forwarded to the tenant's inbound alias) fetches the private tier. The paste dialog is now portal-neutral ("Paste Trust Center Access Link"). See [Trust Center Extraction](/vendor-risk/trust-center)
- Engagement list — the **Owner** column now names the engagement owner (was the Business Head) and reassigns inline; a row expands to show each of its assessments and questionnaires with their owners

### Improved
- A per-question portal upload the evaluator could not confirm now stays listed on the question with a **Couldn't confirm** badge and the evaluator's reason, instead of disappearing. Behind it: a single-question upload is retried once before being scored zero, and re-uploading the same file on a question re-checks it rather than being deduplicated away
- Assessments list gains a **Needs Review** chip
- The engagement list shows the SRP submitted date; the admin user list says **Last signed in** rather than last activity

## Week of Sep 7

### New
- **Vendor portal Final check** — *Submit for review* now opens a pre-submission check that runs the same review engine the risk team uses: deterministic rules over closed-form answers plus a bounded contradiction sweep. Unexpected answers and conflicting pairs are pinned as cards; the vendor fixes the answer (the check re-runs on close) or leaves a note that travels with the answer to the reviewer as *Vendor's response (pre-submission)*. The check advises — submit is never blocked — and ends on a confirmation screen ("Submitted to *customer* · N answers"). See [Vendor Portal](/vendor-portal#final-check-before-submission)
- **Control crosswalk** — a questionnaire question can map to SOC 2 criteria and ISO 27001 controls; such a question is judged from the vendor's audit report rather than its "Yes". The review workbench shows a **Controls checked** pane per mapped criterion — in scope, tested, sample size, result — with the auditor's exception text quoted beneath, and an auditor exception drops the row out of bulk approval as its own *Exception* attention item. A question is **Verified** when every mapped control is tested with no exceptions; a per-question rule in the template editor ("Report must also cover the question's specifics") adds the substance requirement back where a control alone is not enough. See [Questionnaire Templates](/vendor-risk/questionnaire-templates#control-crosswalk)
- **Administration → Notifications** — every mail the platform sends, in one place: switch each notification on or off, pick its recipient roles and extra internal addresses, edit the cadence of scheduled mails (days and hour, in the tenant timezone), preview the branded rendering with sample data, and read a tenant-wide email activity trail. Changes from the shipped defaults are marked, with who last changed each rule and a one-click revert. A new **outbound email domain policy** setting drops any recipient outside the listed domains before a mail leaves. See [Notifications](/administration/notifications)
- **Engagement list export** — an **Export** button beside Filters and Columns builds an Excel file from exactly the rows and filters on screen, with a per-export column picker grouped by engagement, vendor, SRP, pre-onboarding questionnaire, Risk Assessment, and questionnaire owners. Every export starts from the default column set — nothing is remembered between exports

### Improved
- The outsourcing report's **Cyber Security Risk** row now takes the worse of its own inputs and the engagement's own completed Risk Assessment rating, so an approved "no incidents" answer can no longer hide a High security posture. The RA appears in the row's reason line and evidence drawer
- The Expired card is gone from the findings dashboard — it did not apply to findings raised through engagements

## Week of Sep 1

### New
- **TPRM Dashboards V1.1** — five standard dashboards (Vendors, Engagements, Assessments with a questionnaire-kind switch, Findings, Outsourcing Tracking) on one shared filter bar. Every bar, slice, and KPI drills into the Engagements, Vendors, or Findings list carrying the same filters, so a dashboard number equals its landing list's row count. Each dashboard exports, and "How these numbers are computed" opens the methodology PDF. The Assessments dashboard's by-assessor charts count questionnaires under their own owner. See [Dashboards](/dashboards)
- **Engagement list redesign** — Category, SRP tier, PQ and RA status with sent date, PQ and RA rating with score, Disposition, and Next due; a tag line under each row carrying every hero chip; search beside the tabs; icon-only Filters (with count) and Columns; a filter sheet covering the whole dashboard vocabulary with an *Applied* strip; the column set remembered per user
- **Pre-onboarding questionnaire once per vendor** — a vendor answers the pre-onboarding questionnaire once and every later engagement reuses it while it is valid. A new engagement whose vendor already has a completed PQ stops at Assessments with a coverage dialog: *Complete pre-onboarding* links to the covering run and onboards with nothing sent, or *Send a questionnaire anyway* opens the scope tree with covered domains locked (re-askable with a reason). A PQ still with the vendor is followed automatically. Validity is a tenant setting (blank = never expires); when set, the annual renewal becomes one questionnaire per vendor. *Start Post-Onboarding Assessment* now previews its scope and recipient before anything is emailed
- **Outsourcing report aligned to the RBI risk definitions** — ten visible dimensions in the regulator's order (Cyber Security, Reputational, Operational, Concentration & Systemic, Strategic, Legal, …), each opening with the RBI definition. Operational composes financial capacity, fraud screening, process controls, and technology failure; Strategic reads the vendor's line exits, ownership changes, and competing products against the tenant's own **strategic goals** — a new Tenant Settings list with *Suggest from public sources*; Legal bands litigation on the respondent side and adds supervisory actions. A one-time **vendor research** pass (public breach record, outages, regulatory actions, ownership events) feeds the rows, with source links in the evidence drawer. An **expired external DD report** is now withheld until a reviewer explicitly accepts it, and every rating that rests on it carries the acceptance as a caveat. See [Outsourcing Risk](/outsourcing-risk)
- **Vendor progress nudge** — while a questionnaire is open with the vendor, the vendor's contacts get a short check-in every Monday and Thursday at 09:00 tenant time (progress so far, days remaining, offer of help), with the business owner and risk desk on CC. Nothing in the first five days after launch, and never for engagements on hold or dropped
- **Monthly EBS supplier list** — on the Engagement Request Tracker, upload the month's supplier export: the preview states exactly what the upload will do (which suppliers get asked, which onboard silently as Cat 2 engagements, which are held back and why), a downloadable row-by-row check, then commit. Auto-onboarded suppliers appear on a new **EBS** tab with the upload audit trail, and as closed requests under *All* with their real creation dates. See [Engagement Requests](/engagement-requests)

### Improved
- Vendor portal — the AI-suggested answer now quotes the source passage, with page reference, beside **Use this** on the question sheet; **show full excerpt** expands it in place
- Every notification greets its sole addressee by first name, and every mail carries a single Reply-To — the first address in the tenant's `tprm` team (put a group address first to reach the whole desk)
- Closing a finding now emails the vendor and the business; an assessment that closes out after onboarding announces itself; a clean review no longer tells the vendor twice. The email trail tags a combined mail against every role that read it, so a business owner filtering by *Business* sees the mails their address was on
- Tenant Settings now covers every setting the platform reads — the engagement request tracker switch, due-diligence reuse window, finding remediation targets by criticality, the business SPOC directory — grouped by category, and renders a setting type it does not recognise read-only instead of failing
- Service Risk Profile — new hero chips (**Cat 1** in red, **UAE Entity**, **ICFR**, **Infosec Required**, **CSCRF**), a no-claim state that shows no verdict chip rather than a false negative, and Enter opens the passkey prompt on sign-in

### Fixed
- Withdrawing a question from scope now withdraws its conditional follow-ups with it, and restoring it brings them back — fixing "Complete failed — 1 question not yet reviewed" on assessments where every visible question was approved, and a portal that asked the vendor a follow-up its parent no longer justified
- Uploading a large EBS supplier export no longer exhausts the server's memory — the sheet is streamed row by row
