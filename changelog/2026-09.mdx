September is about running the programme, not just the assessment. **My Work** becomes the landing page — every analyst's open items in one list, with AI-drafted follow-ups sent from the platform — and engagement requests and questionnaires are assigned automatically by kind and by load. **TPRM Dashboards** reach V1.1, with every number drilling into the list that produced it, and the engagement list is redesigned around what the dashboards report. Administrators get a **Notifications** tab that governs every mail the platform sends, and an **Evidence policy** tab that decides which documents count as attested evidence. On the vendor side, a pre-submit **Final check** catches contradictions before the risk team ever sees them, and a weekday email lists the questions the risk team is still waiting on. Underneath: the DPSC control crosswalk judges control-mapped questions from the audit report's tested controls, the outsourcing report is realigned to the RBI risk definitions, and the pre-onboarding questionnaire is asked once per vendor. The second half of the month turns to the documents themselves: every upload carries a **validity** read from its own dates, an expired or undated file is put to the vendor before it counts, an expired SOC 1 or SOC 2 report stands only with a current **bridge letter**, and the document's type comes from what it is rather than the slot it was filed under. A fourth evaluation mode accepts a complete written answer without evidence, questionnaire **types** become a per-tenant vocabulary, the engagement list shows the stage each in-progress engagement has reached, and the left navigation is regrouped.

## Week of Sep 28

### New
- **SOC bridge letters** — a SOC 1, SOC 2, or SOC 3 report is now current until the end of its audit period, with no extra months. After that, a SOC 1 or SOC 2 is accepted only while one of the vendor's bridge letters has not ended; otherwise the upload is refused with a message that says what to do. In the portal, an empty or expired SOC 1 / SOC 2 row offers **Upload bridge letter**, and a covered report reads "Bridged until *date*" with the letter that keeps it current. The reviewer sees the same badge, with "Audit period ended *date* · kept current by *letter*" on document rows and question evidence. A bridged report is not treated as expired, so answers resting on it are not held for review. A bridge letter is kept on file but never matched to questions, and one whose window has ended is refused. The Evidence policy tab offers **Only with a bridge letter** on the SOC 1 and SOC 2 rows; a tenant that prefers to ask the vendor sets *Ask vendor* there. See [Administration](/administration#evidence-policy)
- **Expected Documents** — the questionnaire template's Document Requirements tab becomes **Expected Documents**: one guidance list of the documents the vendor portal suggests uploading. Nothing on it is required, and validity is no longer set there — it comes only from Administration → Tenant Settings → Evidence policy. New templates start with four (SOC 2 Type II, Privacy Policy, VAPT Report, Policy Documents); delete any or **Remove all**, and with an empty list the vendor sees only Add Document. See [Questionnaire Templates](/vendor-risk/questionnaire-templates)
- **Questionnaire types** — each questionnaire template now carries a **Type**, chosen in the template editor and shown as a badge on the template. The list of types is a tenant setting (Administration → Tenant Settings → **Questionnaire types**): rename any type, add your own, and the Assessments dashboard picker, the engagement filters, and Assignment routing follow the edit. Six types the product behaves on (PQ, RA, Data Localization, Outsourcing, Offboarding, Other) can be relabelled but not removed, and a type still given to a questionnaire or routed to named people cannot be removed until that is changed. The default labels for PQ and RA now read "Pre-onboarding questionnaire (PQ)" and "Post-onboarding risk assessment (RA)". An assessment whose questionnaire is of type Offboarding is named "Vendor Offboarding Assessment" / "Internal Offboarding Assessment" and carries an amber chip
- **Engagement list Status column** — the Disposition column is now **Status**: an in-progress engagement shows the stage it has reached (Intake submitted, SRP submitted, Assessments, Observations, Business approvals, Remediation) and sorts in workflow order; every other disposition shows as before. The list opens on **All**, with tabs ordered All / Onboarded / Not yet onboarded, and the Excel export carries Status in place of Disposition. See [Vendor Engagements](/engagements)
- **Vendor context on the engagement page** — the Overview tab has one **Vendor record** card: security maturity and research first, then *At this vendor* — its other engagements, the questionnaire in flight, last PQ, last RA, next due — and a link to all of the vendor's engagements. When the engagement has no scored PQ or RA of its own, the hero shows the vendor's rating with a "Via *engagement*" link to where it came from. Before the SRP is submitted, the Assessments tab already names the vendor's questionnaire answered on another engagement. Every vendor-name link opens the vendor's Engagements tab

### Improved
- **Weekday questions email** — the mail now copies further up the business side as questions age: the Business Owner once the oldest unanswered question has waited three days, the Business Head from six. An escalated mail is headed "Action needed" with the days open, and every mail closes with an offer of help and how to sign in. Question text is shown whole rather than clipped. The default send time moves from 17:00 to **09:00** tenant time; a tenant that has set its own schedule under Administration → Notifications keeps it. See [Notifications](/administration/notifications)
- Every vendor-facing email subject now names the engagement, falling back to the product or service name only when the engagement has no name
- **Validity months** in the Evidence policy accept **0** — valid until the document's own period end, no extra months — with a maximum of 24. An out-of-range value is refused in the cell and Save stays disabled
- An engagement waiting on its Service Risk Profile now opens on the **SRP** tab — straight after intake and on any later visit — with a short dismissible card explaining why the SRP matters and what it asks. A link to a specific tab still opens that tab
- The monthly supplier list is now called **Monthly Dump** wherever it is named — the Engagement Requests tab, the upload button and dialog, the My Work source label, the follow-up digest email, and the activity history. See [Engagement Requests](/engagement-requests)
- Assessments dashboard — one searchable multi-select questionnaire picker replaces the kind switch. The landing view covers every questionnaire type except Outsourcing and Data Localization, which have dashboards of their own, and says so. A new **Overdue questionnaires** tile counts the engagements behind it, and drills carry the selection the tiles counted. See [Dashboards](/dashboards)
- Findings dashboard — a new **Assessment** grouping shows which assessment raised the findings, by status, and drills into the findings list filtered to that assessment
- Vendor portal — when no answers could be filled and every uploaded document is expired, the completion banner says so and names the file and its expiry date, instead of a plain "no answers could be filled"
- Long documents reach evidence evaluation in full — the per-document text bound rose from 400K to 1M characters, and the privacy-policy analysis (previously 80K) now reads to the same bound

### Fixed
- An expired file already attached to another assessment could be re-sent past an Evidence policy set to *Reject*; it is now refused on the new assessment, and the other assessment keeps its copy
- Evidence policy number boxes — clearing a value snapped straight back to the inherited one, so a new number could not be typed cleanly. The box now keeps what is typed and returns to the default only when left empty
- A Tenant Settings value-and-label list card could show Save enabled with nothing edited
- **Vendor portal Messages on sub-questions** — a question the reviewer asked on a sub-question did not appear in the vendor's Messages tab, and the vendor's reply was filed against the parent. The tab now shows one thread for the whole question family, each message labelled with the question it is about, and a reply goes to the question that has waited longest unless the vendor picks another; when several are waiting, an amber line names them

## Week of Sep 21

### New
- **Answers or audit reports accepted** — a fourth evaluation mode, selectable on the template and per assessment. It does everything audited-reports-sufficient does, and a written answer to a question with an authored expected answer is judged on whether it actually answers the question rather than on evidence. A complete answer is verified; one missing a specific the question names is held with exactly those gaps; a bare "N/A", an off-topic answer, or a follow-up that contradicts its parent's answer is held. A question whose expected answer demands a document is never verified without one. The reviewer reads the verdict as a verdict on the answer — *Answer accepted*, *Answer incomplete*, *Document required* — and the vendor sees "What is missing from your answer". Assessments already in flight keep the mode they were launched with. See [Questionnaire Templates](/vendor-risk/questionnaire-templates#evidence-policy-evaluation-mode)
- **Cite a passage** in the vendor portal — one click puts a passage the vendor is already looking at into their response, with its source: **Cite in my answer** on a text question, **Cite in my notes** on a choice question (the selected option is never touched). Offered on the answer card's excerpt, each row of the Evidence panel, and the suggestion in the question list; the same passage cannot be added twice. Notes drafted in the question list now survive opening the question. See [Vendor Portal](/vendor-portal)
- **Customer asked banner** — the vendor portal's landing screen now says when the risk team is waiting: "*Customer* has asked you 3 questions", with the age of the oldest and a **Reply to questions** button that opens the Customer asked tab. The View Questions button in the header carries the same count
- **Left navigation regrouped** — three blocks: daily work (My Work, Vendor Risk, Vendor Engagements, Engagement Requests), then intelligence, Vendor Database, contracts, and tools, then a **Setup** group (Questionnaire Templates, User Management, Integrations, Notifications, Administration). **Contracts** now holds every contract screen, including **Customer Golden Template** (was Customer Contract Template) and **Vendor Golden Template** (was Vendor Requirements). Drag the sidebar's right edge to resize it, click the edge or press `[` to collapse it; collapsed, hovering the icon rail floats the full menu over the page. The sidebar opens expanded on every sign-in
- **Administration tabs in the URL** — each Administration tab has its own link, so reload and Back return to the same tab. Intake Configuration moves into Administration as a tab, and Audit Log moves to the end

### Improved
- A vendor who acts on a question the risk team asked about — changes the answer, edits the note, marks it not applicable, or attaches evidence — has replied: the action is recorded in the question's conversation ("Changed the answer from No to Yes") and the question stops being chased in the banner, the Customer asked tab, the weekday email, and My Work. Setting an ETA does not count
- My Work — a vendor reply on a question you have since approved or rejected no longer stays under *Waiting on me*; a vendor message after the decision brings it back
- **Document type comes from the document** — the type the platform reads from an uploaded document now decides how it is named, analysed, and held to the Evidence policy; the checklist slot only records what was asked for. A SOC 2 filed under an architecture slot is analysed as a SOC 2, and a policy filed under the SOC 2 slot is no longer analysed as one. Reviewer and portal document rows show the document's own type. Applies to new uploads; where the document's type cannot be told, the slot's type stands
- Document validity — a suggested answer the vendor confirms on a closed-form question is no longer held for review because the report it came from has expired; approving a question releases any upload still held on it for its company name or date; and **Return to vendor** asks for its note in an in-app dialog that names the file
- Review workbench — a row's answer, gap, verdict, and matched documents now run the full width of the row, under the buttons, instead of a narrow column beside them; on a narrow list the controls take the first line. The detail pane's header and close button stay in reach while the page or the pane scrolls, and clicking inside an open row moves the pane to that question
- Inbound mail — a new-vendor thread started by the business ("please initiate the process for this vendor") is now recognised alongside threads started by the risk team, and a vendor domain or engagement name is kept only when the mail itself states it. The weekly closed-PSR report is read directly from its table. An uploaded `.eml` file is decoded the same way as mail received on the intake alias, so its tables are kept
- Intake form — a form opened from a request that has a vendor and a product but no engagement name now drafts the name on open. The draft is built only from the vendor, the product, and the requester's own use case; a name containing anything else is dropped and the field is left blank for you
- Vendor Inventory and Intake Requests show an error with **Retry** when the list cannot be loaded, instead of an empty list
- Findings — the list's Source column uses the same wording as the Source filter; escalations show people's names rather than user ids; a findings-dashboard drill-down now covers exactly the period the chart counted, and date filter chips read as calendar dates

### Fixed
- Dates showing a day early in time zones west of UTC — vendor ETA dates on the review page, the Excel export, and the portal banner, and "Licence valid till" on an engagement request
- Questionnaire Excel export — column widths were shifted one column to the left, leaving the ID column wide and the Question column narrow
- Contract penalties — saving failed on the customer and vendor contract detail pages, and the penalty editor's SLA Type would not keep a selection
- The contract AI assistant panel could not send a message or be closed; its header now shows the contract number and its greeting names the customer
- Create Finding on the review page reported success when the save had been refused; the error is now shown
- Vendor portal — a message that failed to send showed nothing; it now shows an error
- Back buttons could carry the wrong label after using the browser's Back

## Week of Sep 14

### New
- **My Work** — the new landing page. Every user sees their own list — *Waiting on me*, *Waiting on others*, *Coming up* — built from sixteen kinds of work (a questionnaire submitted or gone quiet, an observation report awaiting confirmation, a finding overdue, a business review pending, a due-diligence report missing or expired, a request waiting on triage, a reassessment coming up). Admins and managers land on **Team Work**: summary tiles, who holds what, items nobody owns, and items held past the team's threshold. Every row has a **Draft follow-up** that writes the vendor chase from the engagement's own facts; send it from HigherOrder or open it in Gmail. A Monday and Thursday morning digest with the same figures goes to TPRM managers. See [My Work](/my-work)
- **Automatic assignment** — every new engagement request gets a TPRM owner on arrival: the active analyst with the least open work. Managers reassign from the triage drawer, and the requests list gains a TPRM Owner column and filter (Mine / Unassigned / a person). Questionnaires are routed by kind on every launch path via a new **Assignment** tenant setting — per kind, the engagement owner (default), one named person, or a pool balanced by least open work. Routing never blocks a launch, and automatic assignments send no email
- **Email vendor** from the engagement page's actions menu — opens the same drafting panel as a My Work row, pre-filled with the engagement's questionnaire facts and recent thread; sends under the tenant's identity and files in **Activity → Email**. When nothing is waiting on the vendor, the panel opens with the facts and an empty message and no model call is made
- **Weekday questions email to the vendor** — Monday to Friday at 17:00 tenant time, one mail per engagement listing the questions the risk team has asked in the portal that the vendor has not yet answered (oldest first, up to ten, the rest counted). The button opens the portal on the **Customer asked** tab. Whoever asked is on CC. On by default; switch off or change the time under Administration → Notifications
- **Awaiting vendor reply** — the review page marks each question the risk team asked and the vendor has not answered with a teal chip ("asked 3 days ago") and a new **Awaiting reply** filter pill; My Work lists the same questions per questionnaire under *Waiting on others* with its own team threshold ("Vendor question unanswered", default three days). The **ETA Extensions** pill and card are retired
- **Evidence policy** — Administration → Tenant Settings → **Evidence policy**: a grid of document categories against five rules (validity months, grace days, what happens when expired, when undated, and whether a mention in the document is sufficient), with per-document-type exceptions and an "effective policy for" preview. In audited-reports-sufficient mode, the document types that count as attested evidence now come from this policy instead of a fixed SOC 2 / VAPT list — policies count by default, certificates and attestation letters do not. See [Administration](/administration#evidence-policy)
- **Document validity** — every uploaded document now shows where it stands by its own dates — *Valid until*, *Expired*, or *Undated* — on the vendor portal's document rows, the assessment's Documents tab, question evidence, and the review workbench; a document is no longer called expired by its upload age. Where the Evidence policy says *Ask vendor* (the default for certificates, audit reports, and attestation letters), an expired or undated upload is held before it attaches and the vendor gets a card, "This document looks out of date": **Upload a newer version**, **Use this document anyway** with a written reason, or — only when no date could be read — **The date is wrong**, with the date supplied. The reviewer sees the vendor's statement beside the document and answers in one click: **Confirm the vendor's date** or **Return to vendor** with a note. An answer resting on an expired document is held under Needs review with the reason ("Rests on *file*, which expired *date*") and lifts on its own once a current document arrives. See [Administration](/administration#evidence-policy)
- **SecurityPal trust centers** — supported natively alongside SafeBase: public documents are pulled on discovery, and a data-room approval email pasted on the vendor's Documents tab (or forwarded to the tenant's inbound alias) fetches the private tier. The paste dialog is now portal-neutral ("Paste Trust Center Access Link"). See [Trust Center Extraction](/vendor-risk/trust-center)
- Engagement list — the **Owner** column now names the engagement owner (was the Business Head) and reassigns inline; a row expands to show each of its assessments and questionnaires with their owners

### Improved
- A per-question portal upload the evaluator could not confirm now stays listed on the question with a **Couldn't confirm** badge and the evaluator's reason, instead of disappearing. Behind it: a single-question upload is retried once before being scored zero, and re-uploading the same file on a question re-checks it rather than being deduplicated away
- Assessments list gains a **Needs Review** chip
- The engagement list shows the SRP submitted date; the admin user list says **Last signed in** rather than last activity

## Week of Sep 7

### New
- **Vendor portal Final check** — *Submit for review* now opens a pre-submission check that runs the same review engine the risk team uses: deterministic rules over closed-form answers plus a bounded contradiction sweep. Unexpected answers and conflicting pairs are pinned as cards; the vendor fixes the answer (the check re-runs on close) or leaves a note that travels with the answer to the reviewer as *Vendor's response (pre-submission)*. The check advises — submit is never blocked — and ends on a confirmation screen ("Submitted to *customer* · N answers"). See [Vendor Portal](/vendor-portal#final-check-before-submission)
- **Control crosswalk** — a questionnaire question can map to SOC 2 criteria and ISO 27001 controls; such a question is judged from the vendor's audit report rather than its "Yes". The review workbench shows a **Controls checked** pane per mapped criterion — in scope, tested, sample size, result — with the auditor's exception text quoted beneath, and an auditor exception drops the row out of bulk approval as its own *Exception* attention item. A question is **Verified** when every mapped control is tested with no exceptions; a per-question rule in the template editor ("Report must also cover the question's specifics") adds the substance requirement back where a control alone is not enough. See [Questionnaire Templates](/vendor-risk/questionnaire-templates#control-crosswalk)
- **Administration → Notifications** — every mail the platform sends, in one place: switch each notification on or off, pick its recipient roles and extra internal addresses, edit the cadence of scheduled mails (days and hour, in the tenant timezone), preview the branded rendering with sample data, and read a tenant-wide email activity trail. Changes from the shipped defaults are marked, with who last changed each rule and a one-click revert. A new **outbound email domain policy** setting drops any recipient outside the listed domains before a mail leaves. See [Notifications](/administration/notifications)
- **Engagement list export** — an **Export** button beside Filters and Columns builds an Excel file from exactly the rows and filters on screen, with a per-export column picker grouped by engagement, vendor, SRP, pre-onboarding questionnaire, Risk Assessment, and questionnaire owners. Every export starts from the default column set — nothing is remembered between exports

### Improved
- The outsourcing report's **Cyber Security Risk** row now takes the worse of its own inputs and the engagement's own completed Risk Assessment rating, so an approved "no incidents" answer can no longer hide a High security posture. The RA appears in the row's reason line and evidence drawer
- The Expired card is gone from the findings dashboard — it did not apply to findings raised through engagements

## Week of Sep 1

### New
- **TPRM Dashboards V1.1** — five standard dashboards (Vendors, Engagements, Assessments with a questionnaire-kind switch, Findings, Outsourcing Tracking) on one shared filter bar. Every bar, slice, and KPI drills into the Engagements, Vendors, or Findings list carrying the same filters, so a dashboard number equals its landing list's row count. Each dashboard exports, and "How these numbers are computed" opens the methodology PDF. The Assessments dashboard's by-assessor charts count questionnaires under their own owner. See [Dashboards](/dashboards)
- **Engagement list redesign** — Category, SRP tier, PQ and RA status with sent date, PQ and RA rating with score, Disposition, and Next due; a tag line under each row carrying every hero chip; search beside the tabs; icon-only Filters (with count) and Columns; a filter sheet covering the whole dashboard vocabulary with an *Applied* strip; the column set remembered per user
- **Pre-onboarding questionnaire once per vendor** — a vendor answers the pre-onboarding questionnaire once and every later engagement reuses it while it is valid. A new engagement whose vendor already has a completed PQ stops at Assessments with a coverage dialog: *Complete pre-onboarding* links to the covering run and onboards with nothing sent, or *Send a questionnaire anyway* opens the scope tree with covered domains locked (re-askable with a reason). A PQ still with the vendor is followed automatically. Validity is a tenant setting (blank = never expires); when set, the annual renewal becomes one questionnaire per vendor. *Start Post-Onboarding Assessment* now previews its scope and recipient before anything is emailed
- **Outsourcing report aligned to the RBI risk definitions** — ten visible dimensions in the regulator's order (Cyber Security, Reputational, Operational, Concentration & Systemic, Strategic, Legal, …), each opening with the RBI definition. Operational composes financial capacity, fraud screening, process controls, and technology failure; Strategic reads the vendor's line exits, ownership changes, and competing products against the tenant's own **strategic goals** — a new Tenant Settings list with *Suggest from public sources*; Legal bands litigation on the respondent side and adds supervisory actions. A one-time **vendor research** pass (public breach record, outages, regulatory actions, ownership events) feeds the rows, with source links in the evidence drawer. An **expired external DD report** is now withheld until a reviewer explicitly accepts it, and every rating that rests on it carries the acceptance as a caveat. See [Outsourcing Risk](/outsourcing-risk)
- **Vendor progress nudge** — while a questionnaire is open with the vendor, the vendor's contacts get a short check-in every Monday and Thursday at 09:00 tenant time (progress so far, days remaining, offer of help), with the business owner and risk desk on CC. Nothing in the first five days after launch, and never for engagements on hold or dropped
- **Monthly EBS supplier list** — on the Engagement Request Tracker, upload the month's supplier export: the preview states exactly what the upload will do (which suppliers get asked, which onboard silently as Cat 2 engagements, which are held back and why), a downloadable row-by-row check, then commit. Auto-onboarded suppliers appear on a new **EBS** tab with the upload audit trail, and as closed requests under *All* with their real creation dates. See [Engagement Requests](/engagement-requests)

### Improved
- Vendor portal — the AI-suggested answer now quotes the source passage, with page reference, beside **Use this** on the question sheet; **show full excerpt** expands it in place
- Every notification greets its sole addressee by first name, and every mail carries a single Reply-To — the first address in the tenant's `tprm` team (put a group address first to reach the whole desk)
- Closing a finding now emails the vendor and the business; an assessment that closes out after onboarding announces itself; a clean review no longer tells the vendor twice. The email trail tags a combined mail against every role that read it, so a business owner filtering by *Business* sees the mails their address was on
- Tenant Settings now covers every setting the platform reads — the engagement request tracker switch, due-diligence reuse window, finding remediation targets by criticality, the business SPOC directory — grouped by category, and renders a setting type it does not recognise read-only instead of failing
- Service Risk Profile — new hero chips (**Cat 1** in red, **UAE Entity**, **ICFR**, **Infosec Required**, **CSCRF**), a no-claim state that shows no verdict chip rather than a false negative, and Enter opens the passkey prompt on sign-in

### Fixed
- Withdrawing a question from scope now withdraws its conditional follow-ups with it, and restoring it brings them back — fixing "Complete failed — 1 question not yet reviewed" on assessments where every visible question was approved, and a portal that asked the vendor a follow-up its parent no longer justified
- Uploading a large EBS supplier export no longer exhausts the server's memory — the sheet is streamed row by row
