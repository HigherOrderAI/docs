June was a heavy platform month. The customer-side review surfaces (Findings page, Question Evaluation) open the month; the Vendor Database — the first search-first browse over the vendor catalog — lands mid-month, alongside SSO V1, RBAC V0.1 with custom roles and an Auditor role, and Dashboards V0.1. Risk Scoring moved to Methodology V1: one 0–10 scale, six bands, higher = more risk.

## Week of Jun 29

### New
- Dashboard export — export chart and table data from any dashboard
- Additional dashboard filters and a Findings dashboard

### Improved
- Question evaluation is more resilient: a cross-verification LLM pass now checks that scoring results map to the questions they came from, and repairs any mismatches

### Fixed
- Contract file re-upload after delete/reject no longer blocked by a stale checksum

## Week of Jun 22

### New
- Downloads on the Vendor Portal — evidence and questionnaire download from the portal
- Second-pass document classification: after the classifier picks a document type, a separate LLM review scores whether that choice is justified by the document and flags misclassifications

### Fixed
- Assessment score recomputes when a text-only "additional evidence" is added (previously only re-scored when files were attached)

## Week of Jun 15

### New
- Vendor Database V0.1 — a top-level search-first surface over the vendor catalog. Type-ahead search with auto-suggest surfaces vendors already in the database and opens their profile
- Vendor Database → Intake integration V0.1 — Intake pulls vendor metadata from the database when the vendor is already known
- SSO V1 — single sign-on for customer users
- Manager Assignment in Admin — assign a reporting manager to each user; RBAC uses this for auditor scope
- Assessment lifecycle: assessments can be marked complete, and expired assessments can be extended
- Vendor Portal can resend access codes even after the assessment has been submitted
- Larger PDF/DOCX uploads accepted from vendors; architecture-diagram uploads V0.2

### Improved
- Findings page: subheader added under the table header, AI search box repositioned below the header with filter aligned right, stats cards show totals across all findings rather than the filtered subset
- Questionnaires page: filter, add-question, and add-category buttons stay visible on empty templates; questions and answers minimize to 100 chars with click-to-expand
- Sidebar collapses when you click into any page
- Vendor Requirements and Customer Contract Template pages restructured with sub-page navigation, inline edit/delete on hover, cleaner tables
- Overall risk analysis reprocesses automatically when a VAPT or Questionnaire document is reprocessed
- Vendors can only submit an assessment once all questions are answered

### Fixed
- Intake soft-delete: deleted intake records are recoverable and no longer collide with new submissions
- Dropbox integration OAuth-return flow landed on the wrong step
- Findings type-mismatch bug

## Week of Jun 8

### New
- Findings Detail page V0.1 — click into any finding to see full context; add a finding manually
- RBAC V0.1 — custom role support with granular permissions; Auditor role included
- Team Assignments V0.1 — assign vendors, assessments, and findings to specific teammates
- Risk Scoring Methodology V1 — one 0–10 scale, six bands, higher = more risk (previously grade-based, mixed scales)
- Dashboards V0.1 — the first cut of a dashboards surface
- Intake form: "Risk Score" renamed to "Assessment Tier" to align with the new methodology

### Improved
- Risk coverage displayed as points-vs-band rather than a letter grade

### Fixed
- Delete buttons on vendor rows no longer trigger the row-open action
- Integrations tab reopens correctly after connector OAuth redirect
- Dropbox "Edit Configuration" closes the dialog instead of editing — fixed
- Intake URLs use the numeric intake_id, not request_ref
