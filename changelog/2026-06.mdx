June opens with the customer-side review surfaces. The new Findings page centralises risk findings produced across assessments, and Question Evaluation lets reviewers walk a vendor's answers with an AI Review dialog that proposes an ideal answer and highlights what the vendor's response is missing. Vendor Portal moves to V0.5 with on-demand access codes, broader upload formats, and a size cap.

## Week of Jun 1

### New
- Findings page V0.1 — a central view for risk findings surfaced from vendor assessments
- Customer Question Evaluation V0.1 — reviewers see each vendor answer alongside an AI-proposed ideal answer and a list of missing points, with edits saved back to the assessment
- Vendor Portal V0.5 — vendors can request a new access code on demand; uploads now accept JPEG and XLS; per-file size cap of 5 MB

### Improved
- "Vendor Questionnaire" renamed to "Vendor Assessments" across the product
- Document delete is now soft delete across the board — failed and rejected documents included. Reuploading a previously deleted document restores its prior analysis instead of re-running it
- Vendor inputs are blocked once an assessment is closed
- Excel questionnaire templates support up to 300 questions

### Fixed
- Duplicate vendor-assessment IDs that could appear after an old entry was deleted
