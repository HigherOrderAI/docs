title: "October 2026"
description: "Jira ticket intake, engagement statuses, reviewed PQ launch, Threat Intelligence, Security Risk results, portal Add documents, vendor rename"

October is about the engagement's life after the questionnaire goes out. Engagements gain **business statuses** — POC Stage, Not Selected, Active, To Be Terminated — with supporting documents and a shared note behind every decision, and the pre-onboarding questionnaire now waits for a **reviewed launch** instead of sending itself. A post-onboarding cycle stays **In Remediation** until its findings close, and the EDD team's **Security Risk** results sit beside the Risk Assessment as a Cumulative RA Risk Rating. Requests arrive from **Jira** — uploaded as a ticket export, or fetched daily with engagements created automatically. The review workbench is reworked around the vendor's answer, with the agent's analysis set apart and a chip wherever the vendor changed something. On the vendor side, **Add documents** takes any number of files and files each one itself, the finding card carries the question and its conversation, and every vendor mail opens the right assessment. A new **Threat Intelligence** page ranks every vulnerability by what has actually happened to it. Underneath: evidence policy defaults that refuse expired and undated documents, a PCI DSS Attestation of Compliance accepted as questionnaire evidence, vendor rename and a delete that no longer destroys the record, and engagement read access for every user.

## Week of Oct 26

### New
- **Threat Intelligence** — a new page under Intelligence, above Threat Campaigns. Every vulnerability and malicious package sits on one urgency ladder named after what has happened: *Exploited → Attack-ready → Exploit published → Critical, no exploit yet → Low*. The ranking is rule-based, with no model call. Tabs: **Relevant** (the default — published in the last 90 days, mentioned by a security source in the last 14, or moved up the ladder in the last 30), **Our stack**, **Since last sync**, and **All**; filters for Published, Technology, Kind, and Vendor outreach; the view lives in the URL, so a filtered feed can be bookmarked. **Our stack** matches threats against a technology list kept under Administration → Tenant Settings → **Threat Intelligence**, and shows how many threats each entry matches. A threat's detail page shows how far along it is with evidence and dates, the affected products, and the four vendor-outreach checks (exploited or attack tools public, common technology, reachable over the network, three or more security sources in 14 days) against a monthly cap of five. Nothing is emailed automatically — a person still creates the threat campaign, and the dialog warns when the checks or the cap are not met. A failed sync is named in orange under the title. The page replaces the *Ingested Threats* tab on Threat Campaigns; **Sync now** starts a sync on demand, and the scheduled sync is enabled per deployment
- **EDD / Security Risk results** — a **Risk ratings** tab on the engagement records what the enhanced due diligence team's Security Risk review produced: status (Not Started, In-Progress, On Hold – PoC Unresponsive, Exempted, Completed), rating, review period, methods, risk drivers, a summary, and evidence files with a comment each — set against the Risk Assessment it was compared with, one result per RA. The engagement gets a **Cumulative RA Risk Rating**: the worse of the latest TPRM RA rating and the latest Security Risk Rating, shown in the header, on the Overview card, and as a **Cumulative** column on the engagement list ("via Security Risk" / "via TPRM RA"). Two new list filters (Security Risk Status, Cumulative RA Risk Rating) and a "Security Risk (EDD)" export group. Anyone who can open the engagement can read; recording, editing, and deleting need engagement *manage*. Every save and delete is audited. The *EDD Required* chip now reads **EDD Applicable**
- **Vendor rename** — admins rename a vendor from the Vendor Inventory (pencil in the new **Actions** column) or the engagement's Vendor identity dialog. Two fields: **Registered name** — the legal name shown across the app and in the vendor's portal — and **Also known as**, a second name that is not shown on screens but still matches uploaded documents to the vendor. A name another vendor already uses, in either field, is refused with the other vendor named. An upload no longer renames a vendor that has a real name: a confirmed document can fill an empty *Also known as* and nothing more
- **Vendor delete keeps the record** — *Delete vendor* now marks the vendor **Deleted** instead of removing it. The confirmation lists exactly what will change: each open engagement moves to Not Selected, in-progress assessments are stopped, open findings are closed. The vendor stays in the inventory with a red Deleted badge, the status filter gains **Deleted**, and the engagement list gains a **Vendor Deleted** filter. A new engagement or a matched document upload on a deleted vendor brings it back
- **PCI DSS Attestation of Compliance as evidence** — a vendor's PCI DSS v4 AoC for a Report on Compliance is read once and expanded into its sub-requirements — In Place, or Not Applicable with the assessor's reason — so it can support the questionnaire answers it covers, and is treated as an audit report like a SOC 2. Only a Compliant, full-assessment, service-provider AoC is expanded; anything else keeps its original text
- **Vendor portal: Add documents** — the vendor drops any number of files onto the Documents card (or picks several at once) and the portal files each one: into the request it answers, or as its own row. A processing area on the card shows each file as Uploading, Analyzing, Waiting, or Done, with "N questions matched so far" and the time left; uploads continue if the vendor moves to another screen. A newer copy of an audit report, certificate, or attestation letter asks **Replace it / Keep both**; a duplicate is pointed at the file it matches; a failed upload offers Try again. Step 2 reads **Review N suggested answers**. *Set ETA* on documents and the Add Document dialog are retired, and Submit waits while uploads are still being sent. See [Vendor Portal](/vendor-portal)

### Improved
- **Review workbench: the answer first** — each question shows two labelled rows, **Vendor answer** and **Expected**, in one shape for every question type, with a **Matches** or **Differs** badge on choice answers and *Not set* where the template defines no expectation. The agent's output moves into its own violet **Agent analysis** zone — one line folded (verdict, then "N not covered"), opening to the points not covered, cautions, reasoning, and matched documents. The answer, the agent zone, and the decision note open independently. "Gap" now reads **Not covered** throughout. See [Vendor Assessments](/vendor-risk/assessments#the-review-workbench)
- **Review workbench: parent context** — a follow-up question whose parent is filtered out of the current list shows a **Parent** block with the parent question and the vendor's answer to it; when the parent is in the list, the header reads "follow-up to 6.3". The tree lines and the empty left gutter are gone, so the question runs the full card width
- **Reply on a completed assessment** — the workbench keeps its message box on any question that still holds an open finding, so a reviewer can answer a vendor's finding update after the assessment is closed. Decisions and everything else stay locked
- **Name-conflict card** — when an uploaded document names a different company, the card asks one question: *Is "X" the same company as "Y"?* **Yes, same company** adds the document's name as the vendor's *Also known as*; **No, but keep the document** attaches it without touching the vendor; **Reject** discards it. A vendor keeps at most two names, so once it has an *Also known as* the card only asks whether the document belongs to it. Renaming from the card needs an admin, and the vendor's Documents tab shows the same question with the vendor's current names
- **My Work** — the *finding updated by the vendor* and *finding overdue* rows now name the finding by the first line of its description instead of leaving it blank
- Vendor portal — Yes / No questions show their options as the same stacked list as every other choice question
- New pre-onboarding runs that merge several questionnaires are named **Pre-Onboarding Assessment** in the portal, mails, and My Work (previously "Vendor Assessment"); existing runs keep their name. The lane word beside an assessment's name is dropped where the name already says it
- A diagram upload whose analysis fails now shows **Failed** with *Try again* on its document row, rather than staying unmatched

## Week of Oct 19

### New
- **Engagement business statuses** — the engagement header chip and the list's **Status** column now carry the business status: *In-Evaluation, Onboarding, On Hold, POC Stage, Not Selected, Pre-Onboarding Completed, Active, To Be Terminated, Offboarding, Offboarded*. **Change status** on the actions menu offers only the moves open from the current status. **Not Selected** is final and needs a reason; its dialog shows what it closes — unfinished assessments, open findings, and the vendor's active status if this was its last live engagement. **To Be Terminated** needs a date and triggers nothing by itself. An engagement becomes **Active** by hand or when its post-onboarding assessment starts. The list gains a Status filter, and each row shows the current step beneath the chip
- **Supporting documents and notes on decisions** — the Change status and Initiate offboarding dialogs accept emails and files (10 MB each), each with a required "What is this?" comment; a failed upload never undoes the decision and can be retried. A new **Notes & documents** card on the engagement Overview holds one shared, team-editable engagement note and every document, each marked with the decision it came with; the Activity tab lists a decision's files beneath it. Notes move out of *Edit details* into this card
- **Reviewed launch for the pre-onboarding questionnaire** — the PQ no longer sends itself when the Service Risk Profile is submitted. TPRM opens the launch from the PQ tab or from My Work and reviews the planned scope in the same dialog the post-onboarding Risk Assessment uses: each questionnaire tagged *Required, Planned, Removed from plan,* or *Added*; a required **justification**, pre-filled and recorded in the engagement history; a due date computed from the selection; and an alert that blocks the launch until the vendor has a contact. The required questionnaire cannot be dropped — narrow its domains instead. Starting a post-onboarding assessment now requires a justification too. See [Engagements](/engagements#stage-4-run-the-assessments)
- **Close assessment has its own moment** — pressing *Close assessment* now sends an "Answer review complete" mail to the vendor (answers reviewed and frozen, what comes next), the business owner, and TPRM (tally, who closed it, a link to TPRM Observations), and the track matrix gains a **Reviewed** step with its date between Received and TPRM Observations. On by default; switch it off under [Administration → Notifications](/administration/notifications)
- **Review-raised findings on the observations report** — a finding raised during the answer review appears on the TPRM Observations report as its own row, badged *Raised in review* with its finding number and due date. It cannot be deselected or removed; its observation, assessor comment, recommendation, and severity stay editable, and a severity change moves the due date. Approval counts only the rows that become new findings and lists the review-raised ones as included as they are. The engagement Findings tab now cites each finding's question number and text
- **Vendor notes** — a Notes card on the vendor profile's Overview: one shared note per vendor that anyone on the team can edit. If someone else saved first, the editor keeps the draft and offers *Use their version* or *Keep mine*. The engagement Overview adds a read-only **Notes on this SRP** card listing every per-question SRP note, with *Open in SRP*
- **Privacy POC and Vendor Manager contacts** — two optional vendor-side contacts on the intake Contacts step and the engagement's Contacts tab. The Privacy POC is recorded for reference and is not emailed about assessments
- **Resend portal invite from the Contacts tab** — a **Resend invite** link on each contact that can be invited (Business, TPRM, and Vendor contacts, the Vendor Manager, and the TPRM Owner) sends a fresh access code for an open assessment using the name and email on file; if the email on file is a placeholder, the popup asks for one and saves it first. The engagement's TPRM Owner is no longer filed as a contact when invited. Any signed-in user can now add, edit, and remove engagement contacts

### Improved
- **Assessments: Not required** — when the Service Risk Profile matches no questionnaire, the stepper shows a grey "Assessments · Not required" with the reason, instead of a green tick; the Assessments and TPRM Observations tabs and the vendor card say the same
- **Post-onboarding: Not required** — when no Risk Assessment is routed for an engagement, *Start Post-Onboarding Assessment* is no longer offered, the tracker reads "Post-Onboarding · Not required", and no next-assessment date is set at onboarding
- **Controls report** — the Risk Assessment controls PDF adds **RA Risk Impact**, a second verdict per control that reflects the reviewer's decision and closed findings; an **Open Findings** section; and **Reviewer Comments** for every control whose RA Risk Impact differs from its Status. The completion date is removed. A multi-select question whose expected answer is "all of them" now reads Compliant when the vendor selected every option. See [Engagements](/engagements#the-risk-assessment-report)
- **Cat 2 track on the Category chip** — for tenants whose pre-onboarding plan defines tracks, a Cat 2 engagement's chip names the part of the PQ it was sent ("Cat 2 + HR", "Cat 2 + Full PQ") on the list, the engagement header, and the export, with a **Cat 2 track** filter. In the same tenant configuration, a Cat 2 engagement with no Risk Assessment routed becomes **Active** as soon as pre-onboarding completes
- **Intake and contact field limits** — every short text box on intake, Edit details, and the contact dialogs enforces its limit with an inline error naming the field, including values filled in from a request or the copilot. Phone numbers are checked: 10 digits, or `+` and the country code
- Service Risk Profile — *Save draft* and *Submit* stay pinned to the bottom of the screen while scrolling, with answered and required-missing counts; the missing count jumps to the first unanswered required question
- Engagement names are shown with consistent capitalisation on the engagement list, the vendor's Engagements tab, and the engagement header
- Contact cards show their edit and delete icons without hovering

### Fixed
- Creating an engagement with an over-long product or service name no longer fails with a server error and a half-created vendor; the form says which field to shorten
- Threat Campaigns — an ingest no longer hangs when a source drops the connection mid-download, and starting a second ingest while one is running reports "Ingest Running" instead of a failure

## Week of Oct 12

### New
- **Daily Jira ticket fetch** — once a day, tickets created or changed in the past week in the projects ticked in the Jira dialog become inbound requests. A ticket with every detail present creates its engagement automatically, owned by the admin who connected Jira until reassigned; one with something missing stays an open request; one that overlaps an engagement the vendor already has is **held for TPRM**. The Jira dialog shows the connected site and a **Ticket Fetch** line with the last run, how many tickets it brought in, or why it failed. The fetch is off until enabled for your deployment, and Jira must be reconnected once so the connection can renew itself. Fetched tickets are labelled "Jira (daily fetch)" on the Inbound Requests tab. See [Engagement Requests](/engagement-requests)
- **Additional documents on an assessment** — a third section on Assessments → Documents where reviewers upload reference files (up to 50 MB), each with a required comment saying why it was added. The files are stored only — not analyzed, not matched as evidence — and can be added after the assessment is completed. The comment stays editable; the uploader, an admin, or a manager can delete
- **Account emails** — a user added with single sign-on now receives a welcome email with a button straight to their identity provider. The password-setup email opens the sign-in page directly on the code and new-password step. Both are redesigned on a HigherOrder letterhead that names the customer

### Improved
- **Every user can read every engagement** — the engagement list and detail are open to every signed-in user in the tenant, with no role required. Changes still need their permissions, and My Work and Team Work keep their own scoping
- **Evidence policy defaults** — out of the box, an expired or undated certificate, audit report, attestation letter, or policy uploaded by the vendor is now refused, with a message asking for a current or dated version; a tenant can still choose *Ask vendor* per category or type. SOC 1, SOC 2, and SOC 3 reports are current for 12 months from the audit period end, and SOC 1 and SOC 2 need a bridge letter after that. Policies are current for 12 months from their own date, with 90 days' grace. Documents uploaded by the customer's own team are never refused. See [Administration](/administration#evidence-policy)
- **Evidence validity display** — a document inside its grace period shows an amber **Grace until** date in the portal and on the reviewer's Documents tab, a bridged SOC report shows its real audit period end, and refusal messages print dates in words ("31 March 2025")
- **TPRM Observations** — observation severity is High, Medium, or Low, and every observation, drafted or added by hand, starts at **Low**: raising it is the reviewer's call, and approving without raising creates Low findings on the Low remediation window (90 days by default). A "Drafting observations…" panel shows between closing the assessment and the draft arriving, and a draft whose AI pass failed says so and offers **Regenerate** instead of "No observations — nothing pending"
- The review note on a decided question can be edited from the full question page, and added where none was recorded
- Vendor portal — a finding card opens from a labelled **Details** toggle rather than a corner chevron

### Fixed
- A date printed with a clock time, or repeated on every page as a download watermark, is no longer read as a document's issue or expiry date
- A document refused by the evidence policy no longer returns the vendor to the same confirmation card in a loop

## Week of Oct 5

### New
- **Upload a Jira ticket** — the *Inbound mail* tab on Engagement Requests is renamed **Inbound Requests**, and its upload dialog starts with *What are you uploading?* — **Email** or **Jira ticket**. A ticket export (XML, Excel, or Word) goes through the same pipeline as a forwarded email; an export with no issue key is refused. The preview shows the issue key, the comments found, the reporter, and the created date, and warns when a Word export could not separate the comments or when the export holds several issues and only the first was read. Re-uploading a ticket with new comments extends the existing request instead of raising a new one. Uploaded rows are badged *Uploaded* or *Jira ticket* in the log. See [Engagement Requests](/engagement-requests)
- **Vendor updated chip** — in the review workbench, a question the vendor has touched since the team last looked carries a blue chip — **New reply**, **Answer updated**, or **New document** — with its age, in every list including Ready to Approve. Opening the question clears it for the whole team and lands on the Conversation tab when the update is a reply. The Analysis tab shows a "Vendor changed this" strip with the answer and note before and after, plus new files, until someone approves, rejects, or replies. A vendor's upload now appears in the question's activity as "Attached *file*"
- **Post-onboarding cycle: In Remediation** — a post-onboarding cycle is complete only when every finding it raised is closed. Until then the progress strip shows it as **In Remediation**, and the *Next assessment* date is held back — it is set when the cycle closes out, counted from the observations' approval date, so a long remediation does not push the next cycle out

### Improved
- **Vendor portal finding card** — the card is titled with the question the finding came from and shows the vendor's answer, the **conversation on that question**, the observation, and the recommendation, with **Open question** to jump to it. A file sent with a finding update becomes evidence on the question — including on a completed assessment — and is downloadable from the thread on both sides. Findings are no longer repeated on the question page. See [Vendor Portal](/vendor-portal#findings)
- **Vendor portal closes at submission** — once the vendor submits, the Documents card stops offering Add, Upload, Replace, and bridge-letter actions; downloads and validity chips stay. Evidence on individual questions can still be attached, because the risk team asks most of its questions during review. When the customer closes an assessment the vendor never submitted, steps 2 and 3 show as done with "Closed — the customer has completed their review" and no Submit button
- **Portal links in vendor mail** — every vendor mail with a portal button opens the vendor's own assessment. The findings reminder, due-soon, and overdue mails go out as one mail per assessment. *Get a new access code* works on a completed assessment
- **Bulk approve** — when some questions in a batch cannot be approved, the dialog ends on an outcome view ("62 approved · 2 couldn't be") with the reason for each. If the whole batch fails, the dialog keeps the batch and the notes for a retry. See [Vendor Assessments](/vendor-risk/assessments#bulk-approval)
- Review workbench detail pane — activity and conversation entries name who wrote them, and a withdrawn domain shows its latest withdrawal reason
- Email copy — a finding's one-line summary no longer cuts mid-word or carries the recommendation; the internal *finding closed* mail includes the reviewer's closure note; when a review ends with findings, the business owner's copy says what action is needed
