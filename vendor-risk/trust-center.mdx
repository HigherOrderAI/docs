---
title: "Trust Center Extraction"
description: "Pull vendor evidence directly from public Trust Centers; handle NDA-gated documents through a one-time email forwarding setup"
---

Trust Center Extraction pulls a vendor's public security evidence — SOC 2, ISO 27001, VAPT, Privacy Policy, DPA, sub-processor lists — straight from their Trust Center portal, without waiting for a questionnaire response. Native support ships for SafeBase, Conveyor, and SecurityPal; other portals (Vanta, Drata, OneTrust, Whistic) are detected and scraped via a fallback anchor extractor. Gated documents are surfaced with an email-based unlock flow: a forwarded magic link for SafeBase, a pasted or forwarded data-room approval for SecurityPal.

## What it does

- Discovers a vendor's Trust Center portal automatically when the vendor is added.
- Extracts public documents into the vendor's Documents tab, tagged with a shield icon indicating the Trust Center origin.
- Recognizes document types and routes them through the same analyzers that handle uploaded evidence (SOC 2, ISO 27001, VAPT, Privacy Policy).
- Handles NDA-gated documents through a one-time tenant setup — sign the NDA once on the vendor's portal, forward the magic-link email, and subsequent fetches unlock automatically (SafeBase).
- Fetches a SecurityPal data room from the vendor's approval email — pasted on the vendor's Documents tab, or forwarded to the tenant's inbound alias — without ever signing an NDA on the tenant's behalf.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discovery">
    When a vendor is added (or on manual re-fetch), the discovery service probes the vendor's domain for a Trust Center portal. Seven portal platforms are recognized: SafeBase, Conveyor, SecurityPal, Vanta, Drata, OneTrust, Whistic. The discovered portal is recorded on the vendor and shown as a badge on the Documents tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Extraction">
    SafeBase, Conveyor, and SecurityPal are extracted with native recipes that read the portal's structured data (for SecurityPal, the profile the page serves — public documents download directly, the rest are NDA-gated). Other portals fall through to an anchor extractor that harvests document links from the HTML.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bucketing">
    Extracted items are sorted into three buckets:
    - **Public direct download** — fetchable immediately
    - **NDA gated** — requires an NDA sign-through on the vendor's portal
    - **Metadata only** — the portal exposes the document name but not a downloadable file
  </Step>
  <Step title="Processing">
    Public downloads are queued through the standard document pipeline — classifier picks the type, then the appropriate analyzer runs. The document appears in the vendor's Documents tab with a shield icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="NDA unlock (one time)">
    For NDA-gated documents, an admin completes the one-time email-forwarding setup for the tenant (see below). After that, the platform automatically triggers the NDA sign-through on the vendor's portal, the magic-link email is forwarded, and the document unlocks and enters the pipeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recognized document types

The anchor extractor and classifier route documents into these categories:

- SOC 2 (Type I and Type II)
- SOC 3
- PCI DSS
- ISO 27001 (and variants 27017, 27018, 9001, 42001 — surfaced but only 27001 has a dedicated analyzer)
- Privacy Policy
- DPA (Data Processing Agreement)
- Sub-processors list
- Penetration Test / VAPT report
- GDPR-related documents
- Terms of Service

Non-analyzable items (SOC bridge letters, marketing whitepapers, ISO variants without dedicated analyzers) are skipped before processing to avoid noise.

## NDA-gated documents

NDA-gated documents are not downloaded automatically. Instead they appear as **metadata only** in the Documents tab with a badge indicating an NDA is required.

### One-time setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Trust Center Setup">
    From a vendor with NDA-gated documents pending, open the Trust Center Setup dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the NDA and forward the email">
    Sign the NDA on the vendor's portal using the tenant's Trust Center email address. When the magic-link unlock email arrives in that mailbox, forward it to the HigherOrder inbound alias shown in the dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verified">
    The setup dialog confirms with a **Verified** badge. From this point forward, the platform can auto-trigger relogin for any NDA-gated document at this vendor's portal and unlock new documents without another manual forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After setup, NDA-gated documents that were previously **metadata only** move into the processing queue on the next fetch cycle.

### SecurityPal data rooms

SecurityPal has no magic link to relogin with; access to the private tier is a **data-room approval** the vendor grants per request. Request access on the vendor's Assurance Profile using the tenant's Trust Center email address. When the approval email arrives:

- **Paste the link** — on the vendor's Documents tab, **Paste Trust Center Access Link** takes the link from the approval email (tracking wrappers from SES or Outlook are unwrapped automatically). The platform lists the data room, downloads every document it is permitted to, and records the portal on the vendor.
- **Or forward the email** to the tenant's inbound alias. The platform binds the email to the vendor whose recorded portal matches — exactly one match wakes or creates the fetch; no match, or several, leaves the email unmatched rather than guessing, and a paste on the right vendor resolves it for next time.

The platform never signs an NDA: a data room whose NDA is unsigned is parked as *NDA signature required* until a person signs it on the portal. An expired approval link is parked as *link expired — re-request*, since SecurityPal cannot re-issue it. Documents are deduplicated by the portal's own document id (every download is freshly watermarked, so file checksums differ).

## Where you see it in the product

- **Vendor Profile → Documents tab.** Trust Center-sourced documents carry a shield icon next to the filename. Everything else about them (reprocess, delete, download) behaves the same as an uploaded document.
- **Vendor Risk Report.** Trust-Center evidence feeds the vendor's risk band exactly like uploaded evidence — the source affects the badge, not the weight.
- **Trust Center Setup dialog.** Per-tenant, one-time setup for the NDA email flow.

## Limitations

- Native extraction is only fully reliable for **SafeBase**, **Conveyor**, and **SecurityPal**. Other portals (Vanta, Drata, OneTrust, Whistic) fall through to the generic anchor extractor — results vary by portal implementation.
- The NDA email-forwarding flow assumes the vendor's portal uses magic-link authentication (SafeBase). Portals with SSO, MFA, or account-based login are not supported by the auto-relogin flow; SecurityPal approvals must be re-requested when they expire.
- Approval emails wrapped by opaque link trackers (Brevo, Mimecast, SendGrid) are not unwrapped; paste the destination link instead.
- No customer-facing job-status UI. Extraction runs in the background; documents appear in the Documents tab asynchronously.
- Documents that require signed NDAs the tenant does not want to sign remain **metadata only** — there is no way to force a public download of an NDA-gated document.
