**HigherOrder runs the full vendor risk assessment workflow that, until recently, only the largest security teams could afford to run — automated and continuous, on every third party.**

If you work in vendor risk, the workflow is familiar. A team requests a new vendor. You read the SOC 2 until your eyes blur, dig through the ISO 27001 SoA, send the security questionnaire, wait two weeks for the response, scan the contract for SLAs and indemnities, file the assessment, move on. The next renewal cycle, you do most of it again. The vendors you can actually assess deeply are the handful you have time for; the rest get a tier and a checkbox.

HigherOrder runs that workflow on every vendor, end to end. Documents come in — SOC 2, ISO 27001, VAPT reports, security questionnaires, privacy policies, DPAs, signed contracts, architecture diagrams — and the platform extracts evidence, builds a threat model based on how the vendor will be used, identifies the controls that need to be in place, generates follow-up questions for the gaps, and produces a risk band with a downloadable report. It happens in hours, not weeks, and it stays current as new evidence arrives.

## How HigherOrder is different

**End-to-end, not a point capability.** Most TPRM tools handle one slice — questionnaires, or contract analysis, or continuous monitoring. HigherOrder runs the full workflow as one connected assessment: intake, evidence ingestion, threat modeling, control identification, follow-up generation, and ongoing reassessment.

**Threat-model-driven, not questionnaire-driven.** A generic security questionnaire treats every vendor the same. HigherOrder builds a STRIDE threat model per vendor based on how the vendor will actually be used in your environment, then identifies the controls that need to be in place for that specific risk profile. This is the kind of analysis that historically required an in-house security architect; the platform produces it for every vendor automatically.

**Multi-modal evidence.** SOC 2 reports, ISO 27001 certificates and Statements of Applicability, VAPT reports, security questionnaires, privacy policies, DPAs, signed contracts, and architecture diagrams all feed the same assessment. Risk bands reflect the full evidence picture, not whichever artifact happened to land in the questionnaire response.

**Contractual obligation extraction.** Most vendor risk tools stop at security artifacts. HigherOrder extracts obligations, SLA metrics, penalties, and contacts directly from your signed contracts — and cross-references them against the commitments you've made to your own customers, so vendor coverage gaps surface automatically.

**Continuous, not point-in-time.** Assessments don't expire on a yearly cadence. As new evidence arrives — a renewed SOC 2, an updated DPA, a fresh VAPT — the platform re-evaluates the vendor and updates the band. The portfolio you saw last quarter isn't the portfolio you have today.
